Mumblew — onboundary reliability diagnostic

Standalone test page. No camera, no MediaPipe — this only tests whether the browser's speech engine reports word-boundary events reliably. Open this on the mobile device/browser where the highlighter freezes.

Setup

Pick a LOCAL voice (same restriction the real app uses) and tap the button once first — mobile browsers require a real tap before speech is allowed to start.

Test A — main window, single utterance

Speaks a 14-word sentence directly through the page's own speechSynthesis. This is the simplest possible case.

Test B — fresh iframe, single utterance

Same sentence, but spoken through a freshly-created hidden iframe's speechSynthesis — this mimics exactly what the real app does on every TTS-engine recycle (Phase 9a).

Test C — 8 calls in a row, iframe recycled every 6

Simulates a real reading session: 8 short utterances spoken back-to-back, with a brand-new iframe swapped in before call 1 and again before call 7 — identical timing to the app's IFRAME_RECYCLE_EVERY_N_CALLS=6. Watches whether boundary events specifically degrade right after a recycle.
