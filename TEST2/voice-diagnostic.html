Mumblew — Voice Diagnostic Harness

Isolated test page, no camera/mouth-tracking involved. Tests every voice your browser reports against the three behavioral contracts Mumblew's speech architecture relies on:

  • Cancel-reliability — does speechSynthesis.cancel() actually stop that voice mid-utterance within a bounded time? (If not: this is the "keeps talking after mouth closes" bug.)
  • Boundary-accuracy — does onboundary fire once per word, with charIndex increasing correctly? (If not: this is the "highlight snaps back to the same word" bug.)
  • Rate-honoring — does doubling utterance.rate actually roughly halve speaking duration for that voice? (If not: this is the "speed doesn't match my calibration" bug.)

Uses the same iframe-recycle trick as the main app (every 6 speak() calls) to avoid the already-confirmed Chromium call-count freeze contaminating these results with an unrelated bug.
